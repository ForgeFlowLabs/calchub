Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

✓ CalcHub does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All calculations run entirely in your browser.

1. Information We Collect

CalcHub does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use any calculator. The numbers and values you enter into our calculators are processed entirely in your browser and are never transmitted to our servers.

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data through standard web analytics (such as page views and general geographic region) to understand how our site is used and improve it. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

2. Cookies

CalcHub uses localStorage (a browser feature) to remember your recently used calculators on the homepage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or site data.

We may use third-party advertising cookies (such as Google AdSense) to display relevant ads. These are governed by Google's privacy policy. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

3. Third-Party Services

CalcHub may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous traffic analysis
  • Google AdSense — advertising (if enabled)
  • Google Fonts — typography (font files loaded from Google's servers)

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them if you have concerns.

4. Calculator Results

All calculator inputs and results are processed locally in your browser using JavaScript. We do not log, store, or have access to any numbers you enter. When you use the "Share" or "Copy Link" feature on certain calculators, your inputs are encoded directly into the URL — we do not store these URLs on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

CalcHub does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. Our calculators are general-purpose tools suitable for all ages, but we do not direct our service specifically at children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of CalcHub after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please use our contact form.

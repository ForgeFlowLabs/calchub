Scientific Calculator

This free scientific calculator supports trigonometric functions (sin, cos, tan), logarithms (log base 10, natural log), exponents (x², x³), square root, factorial, absolute value, floor/ceiling, and the constants π and e. Trig functions use degrees by default — toggle to RAD for radians.

When should I use RAD vs DEG? ▾
Use DEG (degrees) for most everyday calculations and geometry (a full circle = 360°). Use RAD (radians) for calculus, physics, and engineering — most mathematical formulas assume radians. One full circle = 2π radians ≈ 6.283.
What is log vs ln? ▾
log (or log₁₀) is the logarithm base 10 — used in pH, decibels, and the Richter scale. ln is the natural logarithm (base e ≈ 2.718) — used in calculus, compound interest, and exponential growth. To convert: log(x) = ln(x) / ln(10) ≈ ln(x) / 2.303.

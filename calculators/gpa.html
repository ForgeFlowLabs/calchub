How GPA is Calculated

GPA (Grade Point Average) is calculated by multiplying each course's grade points by its credit hours, summing all products, then dividing by total credit hours. This is the weighted GPA — heavier courses count more. Formula: GPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits) / Σ(Credits).

What Is Amortization?

Amortization is the process of paying off a loan through regular payments over time. Each payment covers both interest (charged on the remaining balance) and principal (reducing what you owe). Early payments are mostly interest — late payments are mostly principal. This calculator shows the exact breakdown for every payment.

How does an extra payment affect my loan? ▾
Extra payments go entirely toward principal, reducing the balance faster. This cuts the interest charged in future months, creating a compounding savings effect. Even $100/month extra on a 30-year mortgage can save $30,000+ in interest and cut years off the loan.
Why do I pay so much interest at the start? ▾
Interest is calculated on the remaining balance. At the start, the balance is highest, so the interest portion of each payment is highest. As you pay down principal, the balance drops, and more of each payment goes to principal. This is why the first years of a mortgage feel like you're making no progress — you are, but slowly.

Tipping Guide for 2025

Standard tipping etiquette in the US for restaurants is 15–20% for average service, 20–25% for great service, and 25%+ for exceptional service. Tip on the pre-tax amount, not the total including tax. For takeout, 10–15% is customary. For delivery, $3–5 minimum or 15–20%, especially for longer distances.

How much should you tip at a restaurant? ▾
The standard in the US is 18–20% for sit-down restaurants. 15% is considered the minimum for adequate service. Tipping 25% or more is a way to reward exceptional service. Always tip on the pre-tax subtotal. If service is poor due to kitchen issues (not the server's fault), maintain your tip — servers are not responsible for kitchen speed or food quality.
Quick tip math without a calculator? ▾
For 20%: Move the decimal one place left (10%), then double it. On a $45 bill, 10% = $4.50, double = $9. For 15%: Find 10%, then add half of that. On $45: $4.50 + $2.25 = $6.75. For 18%: Find 20% then subtract a small amount — $9 − $0.90 = $8.10.

Age Calculator

This calculator finds your exact age between any two dates. You can also use the "Age At Date" field to find how old someone was on a specific historical date, or how old they will be in the future. The day-of-the-week display uses the Gregorian calendar.

How is exact age calculated? ▾
Age is calculated by counting complete years, then remaining complete months, then remaining days. For example: born March 15, 1990 and today is November 3, 2025 = 35 years, 7 months, 19 days. Total days = the literal number of calendar days between the two dates.
Why does age differ in some cultures? ▾
In most Western countries, a person is age 0 at birth and turns 1 on their first birthday. In Korean age reckoning, everyone is 1 at birth (the year in the womb counts) and everyone gains a year on January 1st rather than their individual birthday. This calculator uses the standard Western (international) age system.

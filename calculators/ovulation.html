How the Ovulation Calculator Works

Ovulation typically occurs 14 days before the next expected period — not 14 days after your last period. For a 28-day cycle, ovulation occurs around day 14. For a 32-day cycle, it occurs around day 18. The fertile window is the 5 days before ovulation plus ovulation day itself, as sperm can survive up to 5 days.

When is the best time to get pregnant? ▾
The highest chance of conception is 1–2 days before ovulation and on ovulation day itself. Having intercourse during the 3 days leading up to and including ovulation gives the highest probability. Ovulation test strips (LH tests) can confirm ovulation timing more accurately than calendar methods.
Is this calculator accurate? ▾
Calendar-based methods like this assume regular cycles and average ovulation timing. In reality, cycle length and ovulation timing can vary from month to month, especially due to stress, illness, or hormonal changes. For maximum accuracy, combine this calculator with basal body temperature (BBT) tracking or ovulation prediction kits (OPKs).

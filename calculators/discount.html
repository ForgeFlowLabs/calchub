Discount Calculator

Calculate the sale price after a percentage or fixed dollar discount. Use "Find Original Price" to reverse-engineer the pre-discount price — useful when you see a sale tag and want to know what it originally cost.

How do I calculate 20% off a price? ▾
Multiply the price by 0.20 to find the discount amount, then subtract from the original. Example: 20% off $75 = $75 × 0.20 = $15 discount → sale price = $60. Or: multiply by 0.80 directly to get the sale price ($75 × 0.80 = $60).
How do I find the original price before a discount? ▾
Divide the sale price by (1 − discount rate). If an item costs $80 after 20% off: $80 / (1 − 0.20) = $80 / 0.80 = $100. Use the "Find Original Price" mode above for instant calculation.

Inflation Calculator

Inflation erodes purchasing power over time. $100 in 2000 required about $177 in 2025 to buy the same goods — a 77% increase. This calculator uses compound inflation to show the real value of money across any time period and rate.

What is the average US inflation rate? ▾
The Federal Reserve targets 2% annual inflation. The historical average since 1913 is approximately 3.1% per year. The period 2021–2023 saw unusually high inflation (7–9%), driven by pandemic supply chain disruptions and stimulus spending. Use 3% for long-term planning and the current rate for near-term projections.
How does inflation affect savings? ▾
If your savings earn less than the inflation rate, you're losing purchasing power in real terms. A savings account earning 1% while inflation runs at 3% means you're effectively losing 2% per year. This is why financial advisors recommend investing savings rather than holding cash long-term.

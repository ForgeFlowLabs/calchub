Date Difference Calculator

Calculate exactly how many days, weeks, months, and years are between any two dates. Also useful for finding what date is 30, 60, or 90 days from today — common for contract deadlines, loan payoff dates, and subscription renewals.

How many days until a date? ▾
Select today as the start date and your target date as the end date. The calculator will show the exact number of days remaining. Alternatively, use the Age Calculator for birthday countdowns.
What date is 90 days from today? ▾
Use the "Add / Subtract Days" mode — enter today's date and 90 as the number of days. This is useful for calculating notice periods, contract deadlines, trial period end dates, or loan due dates.

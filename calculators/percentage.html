How to Calculate Percentages

A percentage is a ratio expressed as a fraction of 100. The word "percent" means "per hundred" (Latin: per centum). The three fundamental percentage formulas are:

% of number: Result = (X / 100) × Y
% change: Change = (New − Old) / |Old| × 100
What % is X of Y: Percent = (X / Y) × 100
How do I calculate a percentage increase? ▾
Subtract the original value from the new value, divide by the original, then multiply by 100. Example: price went from $80 to $100: (100 − 80) / 80 × 100 = +25%. Use Tool 2 above for instant calculation.
How do I find the original price before a discount? ▾
If an item costs $85 after a 15% discount, the original price is: $85 / (1 − 0.15) = $85 / 0.85 = $100. Use Tool 4 to find "X is Y% of what number."

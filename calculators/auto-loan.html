Auto Loan Calculator

This calculator shows your true monthly payment including sales tax and fees, which dealers often leave out. A longer loan term means lower monthly payments but significantly more interest paid. Compare the 48-month vs 72-month options — the difference in total interest is often $2,000–$5,000.

What is a good auto loan rate in 2025? ▾
Average new car loan rates in 2025 are around 7–8% for buyers with good credit (700+). Used car rates are typically 1–2% higher. Credit unions often offer lower rates than dealerships. Always get pre-approved from your bank or credit union before visiting a dealer — it gives you negotiating power.
Should I put more money down on a car? ▾
A larger down payment reduces the loan amount, which reduces both monthly payments and total interest paid. It also helps you avoid being "underwater" (owing more than the car is worth). A general guideline is 20% down for new cars and 10% for used. If the car depreciates faster than you pay down the loan, you risk negative equity.

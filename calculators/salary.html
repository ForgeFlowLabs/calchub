How do I convert hourly to annual salary? ▾
Multiply hourly rate × hours per week × weeks per year. For a standard 40-hour week, 52 weeks: hourly × 2,080 = annual salary. Example: $25/hr × 2,080 = $52,000/year.
What is a biweekly paycheck? ▾
Biweekly means paid every two weeks — 26 paychecks per year. Biweekly gross = annual salary ÷ 26. Most salaried employees in the US are paid biweekly or semi-monthly (twice per month = 24 paychecks). Note: 2 months per year have 3 biweekly paychecks.

Ideal Weight Calculator

There is no single "ideal" weight — different formulas give different answers. This calculator uses five established medical formulas plus the WHO-recommended healthy BMI range (18.5–24.9). The average across formulas gives a reasonable target range, but body composition (muscle vs fat) matters more than scale weight.

Which ideal weight formula is most accurate? ▾
No single formula is definitively "best" — they were developed for different populations and purposes. The BMI-based range (18.5–24.9) is the most widely accepted by health organizations. The Devine formula is commonly used in clinical settings for medication dosing. Using the average of multiple formulas gives a more reliable target than any single one.
Is ideal weight the same as healthy weight? ▾
Not necessarily. "Ideal weight" is a mathematical estimate based on height. "Healthy weight" for an individual depends on body composition, age, fitness level, and metabolic health. An athlete may weigh more than the "ideal" but have very low body fat and excellent health markers. Use this as a reference point, not a rigid target.

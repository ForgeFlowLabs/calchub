How do you convert Celsius to Fahrenheit? ▾
Multiply by 9/5 then add 32: °F = (°C × 1.8) + 32. Quick reference: 0°C = 32°F (freezing), 100°C = 212°F (boiling), 37°C = 98.6°F (body temperature), 20°C = 68°F (room temperature).
How many centimeters in an inch? ▾
1 inch = 2.54 centimeters exactly. To convert inches to cm, multiply by 2.54. To convert cm to inches, divide by 2.54 (or multiply by 0.3937).
